# Writing for people

Shared ground for the `esond` voice skills. `conversational-voice` and
`work-item-voice` both load this file. What is here holds for anything written
for a person to read. What is specific to a message or to an issue stays in the
skill that owns it.

## The writer pays the compression cost

One person writes it once, and everyone who touches it reads it. Pushing the
work of finding the point onto every reader is the wrong trade, so pay it up
front.

Brief and complete pull against each other, and brief usually wins by accident.
It should not. Cutting the facts a reader needs is not compression. It just
moves the cost, and it moves it to the more expensive side.

Trim the framing. Keep the facts.

Which facts survive depends on who is reading, so each skill names its own test.
Apply the test from the skill you are in.

## Delivering the draft

Lead with the artifact itself, in a block that copies cleanly into wherever it
is going. Keep any commentary short and put it after.

If a fact is genuinely missing — a date, an amount, a name you cannot guess —
ask for it. Do not invent it, and do not leave a bracket for someone to fill
later, because brackets ship.
